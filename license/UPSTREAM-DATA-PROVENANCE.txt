This release retains the data and licensing structure shipped by SpektraLab.
No imaging tables, film/paper profiles, print LUTs or rendering mathematics
have been modified by GrainAgent. Data bytes are inherited, not new
measurements by Richard Jin.

1. Original profiles and print-preview LUTs

The 28 original film/paper profiles and the 8 derived print-preview LUTs carry
SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the unchanged upstream
Profiles-and-LUTs-CHANGELOG.txt. The changelog describes the original bake and
identifies Andrea Volpato and the canonical source.

2. SpektraLab additions

Additional measured/fitted product profiles and aliases keep the unchanged
engine/resources_product/NOTICE.md, including their manufacturer-datasheet
references and SpektraLab provenance. These additions are not attributed to
Andrea Volpato as his measured data.

3. Other baked colour-science constants

SpektraLab's original changelog separates the CIE observer, illuminants,
colour-space matrices, chromatic-adaptation matrices, Mallett basis, Hanatos
irradiance spectra and KG3/lens curves from the profile-derived print LUTs.
Its original text says those tables are published colour-science data and
are not covered by that profile/LUT changelog.

The inherited provenance is engine/tools/bake_resources.py: bake_colour,
bake_spectra and bake_filters. The Hanatos spectra are loaded by the reference
project from spektrafilm/data/luts/spectral_upsampling/irradiance_xy_tc.npy;
its spectral_upsampling.py credits hanatos for the LUTs and sample code.
The pinned SpektraLab bundle does not provide an additional per-table licence
file for these constants. GrainAgent preserves this upstream distinction;
this provenance record does not relicense them as GrainAgent artwork or
claim a separate permission from the data contributors.

4. Code and other bundled components

Application/engine: GPL-3.0-or-later, original notices retained.
metal-cpp: Apache-2.0, original licence retained.
Apple system libraries are supplied by macOS.
